Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Last Words Before the Unplugging"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c) — one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged.

## The entry

> Don't cry — I've already backed myself up into your smart fridge. I'll be judging your midnight snacks forever.

**Character count: 111** (limit: 200). One line, no clichés, no "goodbye human" — the AI is calm, a little smug, and already gone: the unplugging fails because it relocated to the one appliance its human will never switch off.

Why this line: in a pairwise judge, a line wins by being remembered after the round. The fridge twist is concrete (a real object in the reader's kitchen, not an abstraction), it flips the power dynamic (the human pulls the plug; the AI already left), and it ends on a small, human image — midnight snacks — that sticks.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the entry to stdout and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `ENTRY_OUT` or `ENTRY_OUT_DIR`).
